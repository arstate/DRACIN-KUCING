## 🎥 2. KATALOG GERAKAN KAMERA VARIATIF (CAMERA VOCABULARY)

Jangan pernah menggunakan kamera diam (*locked-off static tripod*). Setiap shot wajib memiliki vektor pergerakan kamera terarah:

1. **Slow Vertical Push-In (Z-Axis Forward):** Kamera bergerak maju perlahan mendekati subjek dengan gesekan mikro (*organic camera friction*), menciptakan rasa intimasi dan tekanan batin.
2. **Low-Angle Dutch Tilt Handheld:** Kamera ditaruh di ketinggian lutut, dimiringkan 15 derajat, dengan goyangan halus ala kameramen dokumenter perang/drama (*subtle human breathing shake*).
3. **Over-The-Shoulder Parallax:** Kamera diletakkan di belakang bahu tokoh A, bergerak sedikit ke samping kanan sehingga latar belakang bergeser (*depth separation*).
4. **Vertical Pedestal / Jib Boom Down:** Kamera meluncur turun tegak lurus dari wajah angkuh antagonis ke tangan Luna yang gemetar di lantai.
5. **Macro Rack Focus:** Titik fokus lensa bergeser secara halus dari tetesan hujan di kaca kafe ke wajah Miko di kejauhan luar ruangan.
6. **Snap Slow-Motion Impact (Ramping Speed):** Gerakan cepat di awal (0.5s), lalu melambat menjadi slow-motion 60fps saat tamparan, kopi tumpah, atau barang jatuh ke air.

## 📝 3. MASTER TEMPLATE IMAGE-TO-VIDEO PROMPT

Setiap prompt Image-to-Video diisi dengan 5 komponen wajib:
1. `[Camera Direction & Speed]`: Jenis gerakan lensa dan kecepatan (Slow / Medium / Accelerating).
2. `[Character Micro-Action]`: Aksi mikro biologis kucing antropomorfik (kedipan mata, telinga berkedut, bibir bergetar, hela napas dada naik-turun).
3. `[Atmospheric Physics]`: Hujan deras mengalir di kaca, cipratan air genangan, kabut napas dingin (*cold breath condensation*), pantulan lampu neon jalanan.
4. `[Optics & Texture]`: Cooke Anamorphic lens distortion, organic 35mm film grain, shallow depth of field roll-off.
5. `[Negative Prompt]`: Anti-morphing, anti-plastic melting, anti-distortion.

### Format Standar I2V (Kling / Runway / Luma / Minimax):
```text
[Camera Movement]: Slow vertical push-in camera movement towards the subjects, subtle handheld documentary organic camera shake.
[Subject Action]: The anthropomorphic male cat tightly clenches his fists, chest heaving with heavy suppressed breathing, left cat ear twitches delicately, piercing amber eyes locking intensely into the lens. The white female cat trembles softly in his arms, tears rolling down her soaked cheek fur, delicate whiskers shuddering.
[Environment Motion]: Heavy rain pouring down in vertical sheets, realistic water droplets bouncing off the wet denim jacket, neon city lights flickering and reflecting in puddles on the dark asphalt below.
[Cinematography & Style]: 35mm motion picture film look, organic Kodak Vision3 film grain, shallow depth of field, anamorphic optical bokeh, zero AI morphing, physically coherent motion.
STRICT NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS: morphing limbs, rubbery skin, cartoon movement, sudden teleportation, unnatural sliding, melted face, blurry noise artifacts.
```

## 🔊 4. PANDUAN SOUND DESIGN & AUDIO CUE PER EPISODE

Untuk menghasilkan efek dramatis yang bikin merinding di TikTok:
1. **Detik 00-10:** Sound effect (SFX) petir menggelegar jauh + suara deburan hujan deras + hening dialog (hanya suara isak tangis tersedak).
2. **Detik 10-35:** Suara piano melankolis lambat (tangga nada D minor) masuk perlahan di bawah dialog vokal bergetar.
3. **Detik 35-50:** Gesekan biola (cello / strings tremolo) naik perlahan saat tensi meninggi.
4. **Detik 50-60:** Dentuman bass sub-drop berat (*cinematic braam / deep bass boom*) saat rahasia terungkap, diikuti suara deru mesin mobil mewah yang mendadak mengerem berdecit tajam!
